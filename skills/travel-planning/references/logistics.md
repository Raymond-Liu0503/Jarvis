## Objective

Prove that the travel critical path works for the specified travelers and local dates, with resilient buffers and dated rechecks.

## Workflow

1. Confirm origin, destination, traveler count, nationality or document assumptions, health and accessibility constraints, and every local date and time zone.
2. Sequence departure, arrival, immigration or security, baggage, terminal or station changes, transfers, check-in, and fixed reservations.
3. Validate minimum connection rules, operator schedules, opening hours, ticket windows, overnight boundaries, realistic rest, and resilient buffers.
4. Identify disruption points and alternatives; set triggers for switching routes or plans.
5. Schedule rechecks for documents, reservations, schedules, weather, advisories, insurance coverage, and accessibility arrangements.

## Evidence

Use border and travel authorities for entry or advisory claims, public-health authorities for health guidance, carriers and transport operators for rules and schedules, venues or lodging providers for arrival conditions, and weather authorities for forecasts. Compare local timestamps and check that all legs describe the same date and terminal. Seek closure, strike, seasonal, or accessibility evidence that could break the path.

## Return

Return atomic findings with local date/time, operator, route leg, minimum and chosen buffer, restriction, retrieval time, expiry, fallback trigger, and unresolved traveler-specific question. Provider data does not guarantee admission or operation.
