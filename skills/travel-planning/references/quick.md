## Use

Use Quick mode for one bounded destination, route, schedule, weather, event, cost, or feasibility question that does not require a complete itinerary.

## Process

Frame the relevant traveler, origin, destination, local date, and constraint. Make the single highest-value retrieval from the official provider or authority for changing claims; treat third-party lists as discovery.

## Deliver

Answer concisely with local time zone, currency, retrieval time, restrictions, availability, and expiry where relevant. State consequential assumptions and provider caveats. Recommend Deep Research when the request depends on a multi-step critical path, comparative trip costing, accessibility coordination, or disruption planning.
