## Objective

Compare feasible trip options on consistent assumptions and expose total cost, restrictions, availability, and sensitivity.

## Workflow

1. Fix dates, origin, traveler count, room occupancy, currency, trip duration, inclusions, and exchange-rate assumption.
2. Build bottom-up totals for transport, lodging, taxes, carrier or resort fees, baggage, transfers, local travel, reservations, food allowance, insurance, and required accessibility arrangements.
3. When evidence permits, compare budget, baseline, and premium proposals using the same scope and state every exclusion.
4. Record fare or room class, refund and change terms, stock status, booking window, provider, retrieval time, and expiry.
5. Test sensitivity to dates, routing, occupancy, exchange rates, cancellation terms, and the largest uncertain estimates; propose a practical fallback when a limit is breached.

## Evidence

Use direct transport and lodging offers for current terms, official fee schedules for mandatory charges, and reputable local sources for estimates. Do not compare teaser prices with all-in totals. Cross-check unusually low offers, provider identity, and date applicability; seek restrictions that could erase the apparent saving.

## Return

Return atomic cost findings, formulas, common assumptions, inclusions, exclusions, option totals and ranges, sensitivities, restrictions, freshness, fallback trigger, and unresolved cost. Never describe observed inventory as held or guaranteed.
