## Objective

Choose traveler-fit itinerary anchors and shape an attainable attendee journey around dates, pace, interests, accessibility, and weather tolerance.

## Workflow

1. Translate purpose, pace, interests, prior experience, mobility, dietary or health needs, and risk tolerance into anchor criteria.
2. Select a small number of fixed-date or high-value anchors before filling flexible time.
3. Verify occurrence dates, location, opening hours, closure days, admission or reservation windows, duration, accessibility, and local transit.
4. Fit anchors into realistic daily geography and energy levels; avoid double-booking and fragile cross-city sequences.
5. Add a practical indoor, low-effort, or no-reservation alternative where weather or availability could materially disrupt the plan.

For an explicit concert, festival, show, or ticket lookup with a stated destination and date window, use the structured events provider before generic place search. Treat returned price ranges as snapshots, not guaranteed ticket inventory.

## Evidence

Use official venue, event, park, tourism, transit, and local-authority sources for operating claims. Use place or event databases for discovery and cross-check against the operator. Confirm that similarly named events or branches match the exact location and date. Seek seasonal closures, sell-outs, accessibility limitations, or local conditions that would disconfirm feasibility.

## Return

Return atomic findings with anchor rationale, local date/time, duration, location, reservation rule, accessibility note, weather dependency, alternative, freshness, caveat, and unresolved preference.
