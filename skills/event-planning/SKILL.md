# Event Planning

## Outcome
Produce an event brief, recommendation, alternatives, milestones, dependencies, budget, risk register, and open decisions.

## Workflow
Define purpose and success; assess audience, format, and feasibility; design the journey; sequence operations; budget; assign risks, owners, communications, and Plan B triggers.

## Decision rules
Set objectives and KPIs before format or venue. Match capacity, accessibility, inclusion, food, sustainability, and technology to the audience. Validate venue, transport, AV, staffing, suppliers, and run-of-show on a critical path. Include fees and contingency; flag contract assumptions without legal advice. Assign owners and fallback triggers. Never reserve, contract, invite, or purchase.
