## Use

Use Quick mode for one bounded venue, format, capacity, schedule, weather, transport, accessibility, supplier-category, or cost question that does not require a full event plan.

## Process

Frame the event type, audience size, location, date, and decisive constraint. Retrieve only the highest-value current venue, authority, transport, weather, or supplier evidence needed to answer it.

## Deliver

Answer concisely with assumptions, capacity basis, inclusions, restrictions, retrieval date, and availability caveat. Surface the next unresolved decision. Recommend Deep Research when strategy, attendee experience, critical-path operations, a bottom-up budget, or an owned risk register is required.
