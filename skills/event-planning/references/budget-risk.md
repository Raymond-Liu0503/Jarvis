## Objective

Bound whole-event cost and convert material financial, supplier, safety, weather, technology, and delivery threats into owned mitigations and Plan B triggers.

## Workflow

1. Build a bottom-up budget by venue, food, production and AV, technology, program, staffing, transport, accessibility, security, permits, insurance, marketing, payment fees, taxes, and contingency.
2. Record quantity, unit, rate, inclusion, exclusion, currency, tax basis, estimate class, retrieval date, and uncertainty for each line.
3. Compare supplier and contract assumptions for deposits, cancellation, attrition, minimum spend, overtime, force majeure, liability, insurance, data, substitution, and change control; flag matters for qualified review.
4. Create a risk register with likelihood, impact, trigger, prevention, response, owner, decision deadline, and residual exposure.
5. Define Plan B thresholds and an emergency communications tree for safety, weather, attendance, supplier failure, venue loss, and technology outage.

## Evidence

Use direct venue or supplier terms and quotes when available, applicable public requirements, route and weather data, and clearly labeled estimates. Compare options on the same scope and expose fees. Seek exclusions, minimums, or correlated risks that could exhaust contingency. Do not interpret contract language as legal advice.

## Return

Return atomic budget and risk findings, formulas, range and contingency basis, contract assumption, trigger, mitigation, owner, residual risk, caveat, and unresolved decision. Never imply a supplier or venue is held.
