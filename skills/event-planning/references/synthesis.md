## Assemble

Turn the brief into a concept, alternatives, attendee journey, operating model, milestones, run-of-show dependencies, budget, and owned risk register.

## Audit

Draft; check objective-KPI alignment, capacity, accessibility, timing, critical path, budget arithmetic, fees, contingency, contracts, risk owners, Plan B triggers, communications, freshness, contradictions, and false availability. Revise once when a material defect is found; do not start an unbounded search. Keep limitations visible.

## Deliver

Map brief and choice to `executiveAnswer`; milestones and decisions to `recommendations`; concepts or venues to `alternatives`; experience, cost, and operations to `tradeoffs`; register and Plan B triggers to `risks`; scope, dates, budget basis, and open decisions to `assumptions`; and atomic support to `findings` and `sources`. Set dated `freshAt`; imply no reservation or contract.
