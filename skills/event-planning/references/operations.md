## Objective

Prove that venue, transport, AV, staffing, suppliers, critical path, and run-of-show can deliver the concept from load-in through load-out.

## Workflow

1. Score venues on verified capacity by layout, usable space, accessibility, location, transport, loading, curfew, utilities, AV, food rules, staffing, weather exposure, and total operating fit.
2. Translate attendance and program into room sets, flows, queue capacity, back-of-house, emergency access, and changeover time.
3. Map suppliers, staffing roles, AV and network requirements, transport, permits or approvals, deliverables, handoffs, and decision deadlines.
4. Build a milestone schedule and dependency-based critical path; assign owners and float to fragile tasks.
5. Draft a timed run-of-show with cues, communications channels, escalation paths, contingency actions, and load-out.

## Evidence

Use current venue technical packs, floor plans, direct policies, transport operators, weather authorities, and applicable public authorities. Do not infer usable capacity from headline capacity. Cross-check dates, room layouts, access windows, and simultaneous requirements. Seek bottlenecks and single points of failure that invalidate the preferred operation.

## Return

Return atomic findings with scorecard criterion, verified capacity or constraint, dependency, predecessor, deadline, owner, buffer, run-of-show cue, fallback, caveat, and unresolved operational question.
