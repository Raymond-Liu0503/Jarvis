## Objective

Design an inclusive program and end-to-end attendee journey that fulfills the event objectives across physical and digital touchpoints.

## Workflow

1. Map discovery, registration, pre-arrival, arrival, participation, breaks, food, networking, departure, and follow-up for each audience segment.
2. Build program blocks around objectives, attention, transition time, room turnover, speaker needs, and recovery buffers.
3. Specify accessibility and inclusion needs for mobility, sensory access, language, dietary requirements, caregiving, faith, safety, and digital participation.
4. Assess food service, waste, sourcing, transport, materials, and energy choices against sustainability goals and operational reality.
5. For virtual or hybrid formats, validate platform capacity, moderation, captioning, connectivity, recording consent, support, redundancy, and parity risks.

## Evidence

Use attendee requirements and applicable accessibility guidance as design inputs; verify venue, caterer, platform, transport, and weather constraints with their direct sources. Treat promotional accessibility or sustainability statements as declarations until supported by specific features. Seek exclusion points, queue failures, dietary cross-contact, and technology single points of failure.

## Return

Return atomic journey findings with audience segment, touchpoint, requirement, program dependency, service level, evidence, fallback, caveat, owner, and unresolved experience decision.
