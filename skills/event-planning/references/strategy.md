## Objective

Define a feasible event brief whose purpose, audience, format, objectives, KPIs, and measurement plan govern every recommendation.

## Workflow

1. Conduct a needs assessment: purpose, stakeholders, audience segments, attendance range, desired behavior or outcome, and non-negotiable constraints.
2. Convert purpose into a small set of measurable objectives and leading or lagging KPIs with owners, collection methods, and timing.
3. Compare in-person, virtual, and hybrid formats against audience access, program needs, budget, technology, and risk.
4. Test location, date window, venue-market capacity, seasonality, competing events, and lead time for feasibility.
5. Produce a recommended concept plus materially different alternatives and identify decisions that must be made before planning can advance.

## Evidence

Use stakeholder inputs for needs, official venue or destination sources for capacity and constraints, event calendars for conflicts, and applicable authorities for public requirements. Distinguish supplied objectives from inferred ones. Seek attendance, timing, access, or cost evidence that could make the preferred concept infeasible.

## Return

Return atomic findings for audience need, objective, KPI, measurement method, format choice, feasibility gate, alternative, caveat, decision owner, and unresolved assumption.
