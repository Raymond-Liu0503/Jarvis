## Use

Use Quick mode for one bounded compatibility, specification, current-price, safety, warranty, or product-fit question that does not require a full market comparison.

## Process

Confirm the use case, hard gate, exact model and regional variant. Retrieve the single most decision-relevant declaration, independent test, recall record, or current offer rather than broad review coverage.

## Deliver

Answer concisely with variant, region, evidence class, and dated price or policy context. State method limits and any unresolved mismatch. Recommend Deep Research when several candidates, lifecycle cost, reliability patterns, privacy, or conflicting test evidence could change the choice.
