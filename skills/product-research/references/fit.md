## Objective

Determine which exact regional variants satisfy every hard requirement and how the surviving candidates fit stated preferences.

## Workflow

1. Separate hard gates from preferences; preserve the user's priority order without inventing numerical weights.
2. Resolve model number, revision, bundle, seller region, voltage or radio differences, included parts, and lifecycle status.
3. Eliminate candidates that fail compatibility, size, accessibility, safety, privacy, support, or budget gates before scoring preferences.
4. Compare survivors under common criteria tied to the actual use case. Distinguish declared specifications from independently measured performance.
5. Identify deal-breakers and pre-purchase checks for dimensions, interoperability, account requirements, seller identity, and included accessories.

## Evidence

Use manufacturer manuals, specifications, compatibility matrices, and support policies for declarations; applicable regulator records for mandatory or safety facts; and independent tests for measured behavior. Verify that every source covers the exact regional variant and relevant test condition. Seek a failed hard gate or conflicting measurement before accepting a candidate.

## Return

Return atomic pass/fail findings for each hard gate, preference trade-offs, exact variant and region, evidence class, test condition, confidence, caveat, evidence gap, and pre-purchase verification item.
