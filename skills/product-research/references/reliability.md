## Objective

Assess measured performance, recurring failure modes, safety, privacy, support longevity, and the strength and relevance of the available evidence.

## Workflow

1. Classify each claim as manufacturer declaration, standardized test, independent test, representative owner evidence, anecdote, or marketing.
2. Evaluate protocol relevance, sample size, controls, unit and firmware version, repeatability, and whether the metric predicts the user's experience.
3. Compare independent results using common conditions where possible; preserve material disagreement instead of averaging incompatible tests.
4. Check applicable recalls, safety notices, privacy practices, update commitments, warranty exclusions, repairability, parts support, and end-of-life signals.
5. Use owner evidence only for repeated patterns; check variant mixing, incentives, suspicious bursts, duplicated language, and selection bias.

## Evidence

Prioritize regulator recall or safety records, disclosed-method independent testing, support and warranty policies, credible repair data, then corroborated long-term owner patterns. Treat ratings and isolated anecdotes as weak signals. Seek negative results, known failure conditions, and evidence from later ownership periods.

## Return

Return atomic findings with evidence class, exact variant, method and sample limits, repeatability, failure frequency only when supported, firmware or date context, confidence, contradiction, caveat, and unresolved long-term risk. Do not imply Jarvis performed hands-on testing.
