## Assemble

Apply hard gates first, compare exact regional variants under common criteria, then recommend conditionally. Explain value tiers, evidence classes, ownership cost, deal-breakers, and verification tasks.

## Audit

Draft; check variant and region, hard gates, weights, common criteria, calculations, test relevance, recalls, privacy, updates, seller and warranty caveats, contradictions, and hands-on claims. Revise once when a material defect is found; do not start an unbounded search. Keep remaining limitations visible.

## Deliver

Map best fit to `executiveAnswer`; selection and checks to `recommendations`; value tiers to `alternatives`; preference and cost sensitivity to `tradeoffs`; deal-breakers, safety, reliability, privacy, and support to `risks`; region, horizon, and gaps to `assumptions`; and atomic support to `findings` and `sources`. Set dated `freshAt`; do not imply purchase.
