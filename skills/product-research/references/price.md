## Objective

Compare current and whole-life value for exact candidates under a common ownership scenario and identify credible value-tier alternatives.

## Workflow

1. Fix variant, region, seller, currency, tax and shipping basis, stock status, ownership horizon, and required use pattern.
2. Validate seller authenticity and record return window, restocking terms, warranty coverage and exclusions, and support region.
3. Calculate purchase cost plus required accessories, subscriptions, consumables, maintenance, expected energy, repair exposure, and plausible replacement timing.
4. Compare entry, mid, and premium alternatives only after confirming the same hard gates and cost scope.
5. Test sensitivity to ownership horizon, usage, consumable rate, subscription changes, energy assumptions, and uncertain repair costs.

## Evidence

Use authorized manufacturer or seller pages for current offers and terms, official warranty and return documents for coverage, and credible energy, repair, or consumable data for lifecycle estimates. Cross-check unusually low prices and marketplace seller identity. Seek exclusions or recurring costs that could reverse the apparent value ranking.

## Return

Return atomic price and policy findings, formulas, inclusion basis, lifecycle range, option tier, sensitivity, seller caveat, retrieval time, expiry, and unresolved cost. Never claim a guaranteed lowest price, authentic seller, or available unit without verification.
