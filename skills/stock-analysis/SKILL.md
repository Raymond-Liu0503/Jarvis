# Stock Analysis

## Outcome
Produce a dated, decision-ready thesis with a variant view, operating drivers, valuation range, catalysts, monitoring indicators, and explicit invalidation evidence.

## Workflow
Set the mandate and horizon; identify the exact security; map the business and industry; normalize history and build a KPI driver tree; forecast; value the security; test the expectations gap; then assess catalysts, risks, and thesis invalidation.

## Decision rules
Require comparable periods, currencies, and definitions. Verify management claims against filings; test earnings quality, capital structure, and dilution. Select peers by economic exposure, not label alone. Use traceable base, bull, and bear cases; add sensitivities and reverse valuation only when inputs support them. Expose uncertainty and keep the result informational.
