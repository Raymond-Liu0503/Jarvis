## Use

Use Quick mode for one bounded concept, filing fact, price-context question, or clearly scoped comparison that does not require an integrated forecast and valuation.

## Process

Confirm the exact security and relevant fiscal period, currency, as-of date, and horizon. Retrieve only the highest-value current or primary evidence needed for the claim; use the latest applicable filing for issuer facts and dated market data for price claims.

## Deliver

Answer concisely with definitions and calculations visible. Label delayed data, inconsistent metric definitions, and unresolved security ambiguity. Recommend Deep Research when the decision depends on normalized history, a driver forecast, peer selection, scenarios, or thesis invalidation.
