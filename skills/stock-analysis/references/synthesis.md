## Assemble

Connect the exact security, mandate, horizon, business map, normalized history, drivers, forecast cases, valuation, expectations gap, catalysts, and risks. Lead with the thesis and variant view.

## Audit

Draft; check security identity, comparable periods, calculations, case consistency, dilution, obligations, peer logic, catalyst timing, contradictions, freshness, and false precision. Revise once when a material defect is found; do not start an unbounded search. Keep remaining limitations visible.

## Deliver

Map thesis and variant view to `executiveAnswer`; actions and indicators to `recommendations`; cases or peers to `alternatives`; sensitivities to `tradeoffs`; downside and invalidation to `risks`; scope and gaps to `assumptions`; and atomic support to `findings` and `sources`. Set dated `freshAt`; keep it informational.
