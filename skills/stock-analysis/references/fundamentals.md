## Objective

Establish the issuer's business economics, normalized operating history, KPI driver tree, earnings quality, capital structure, and supportable forecast.

## Workflow

1. Resolve issuer, listing, share class, filing version, reporting currency, fiscal calendar, and comparison periods.
2. Map segments, customers, geography, industry structure, revenue model, cost drivers, and capital intensity.
3. Normalize history for restatements, acquisitions, disposals, accounting changes, seasonality, and GAAP/non-GAAP definition changes.
4. Reconcile revenue, margins, working capital, operating cash flow, free cash flow, stock compensation, dilution, debt, obligations, and capital allocation.
5. Build a driver tree from operational KPIs to financial results. Forecast only drivers supported by comparable history or explicit assumptions; expose dependencies across base, bull, and bear cases.

## Evidence

Use regulator filings and notes for reported facts, then issuer releases and presentations for declared KPIs, then reconciled datasets for cross-checking. Verify consequential management claims against reported outcomes and comparable periods. Investigate unexplained reconciliation gaps and seek evidence against the assumed driver relationship.

## Return

Return atomic findings with period, unit, definition, calculation, source support, forecast assumption, confidence, caveat, and unresolved data question. Flag earnings-quality concerns and inputs that cannot be made comparable.
