## Objective

Establish benchmark-relative market behavior, current expectations, dated catalysts, and measurable indicators that would confirm or weaken the thesis.

## Workflow

1. Resolve the exact security, venue, currency, session, observed move, horizon, and as-of time.
2. Compare returns and valuation context with an appropriate broad index, industry benchmark, and economically relevant peers.
3. Separate confirmed company events, macro or industry changes, analyst interpretation, rumors, and causal inference.
4. Test whether event timing and magnitude plausibly explain the move; preserve credible competing explanations.
5. Identify scheduled and conditional catalysts, what the market appears to expect, and indicators to monitor after each catalyst.

## Evidence

Use exchange or market data for prices, regulator and issuer sources for events, official economic or industry data for external drivers, and reputable reporting for context. Check timestamps, market sessions, corporate actions, and revised announcements. Seek a benchmark or contrary event that could disconfirm the proposed attribution.

## Return

Return atomic, dated findings with security, benchmark, period, calculation, catalyst status, expected observation window, confidence, caveat, and alternative explanation. Mark quotes, consensus data, and news as expiring snapshots.
