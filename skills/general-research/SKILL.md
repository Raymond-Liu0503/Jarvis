---
name: general-research
description: Research broad factual, explanatory, comparative, or current-information questions that do not fit a more specialized installed skill.
---
Frame the question, decision, definitions, and scope. Identify material unknowns; ask only when an answer would change the work, otherwise state assumptions. Match sources to claims, separate evidence from inference, and stop when consequential claims have suitable independent coverage. Preserve real contradictions without manufacturing false balance, and disclose unresolved uncertainty.

Use [quick](references/quick.md) for bounded answers. Deep Research loads only the selected [evidence](references/evidence.md), [context](references/context.md), or [verification](references/verification.md) lens, then [synthesis](references/synthesis.md).
